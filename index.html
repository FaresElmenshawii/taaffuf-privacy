Privacy policy for Taaffuf

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Taaffuf is a browser extension that blocks unwanted websites and words and blurs people in pictures and videos.

What we collect

Nothing. Taaffuf has no server and no account. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any information about you or your browsing.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings and your own lists (blocked sites, words, channels and allowed sites) are saved in Chrome's storage. If Chrome sync is on, Chrome copies them to your own Google account so they follow you to your other computers. We cannot see them.
  • A count of blocked pages and the addresses of recently blocked pages are saved on your device only, to show your dashboard. You can clear them by removing the extension.
  • If you set a settings password, only a scrambled (hashed) form of it is saved, on your device and in your Chrome storage.

How pictures and pages are checked

Pictures, video frames, page addresses and page text are checked inside your browser by code and a model that ship with the extension. Nothing is uploaded.

Settings file

If you export your settings, the file is saved where you choose. It contains your lists, your settings and the recently blocked page addresses. It never contains your password.

Permissions

The extension can read the pages you visit because that is how a content filter works. This access is used only for the checks described above.
